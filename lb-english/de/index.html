Andere Frage stellen →
Bist du wirklich der einzige Lehrer?

Yes. Every 1:1, every correction, every Speaking Club is led by me. That’s the point — you don’t get bounced between tutors. If demand exceeds quality, I close enrolment. Scholar & Elite are capped.

Wie unterscheidet sich das von Preply / italki?

Marketplaces optimize for volume. I optimize for outcome. You get a diagnostic, a tailored roadmap, and resources written for your errors — not a library of PDFs. And I stay with you until your exam.

Was wenn Prüfung in 3 Wochen?

Book the free consult immediately. If it’s feasible, we’ll do a crash plan (daily feedback). If not, I’ll give you an honest timeline and free resources to start — I won’t sell you false hope.

Kann ich jederzeit kündigen?

Yes, in one click. No lock-in. You keep all downloaded resources. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata within 30 days.

Unterrichtest du Kinder?

Yes — ESL for ages 12+ and young adult exam prep. For under-12s, I offer parent consultancy and referrals to trusted partners.

Verfügbarkeit?

Live teaching GMT 08:00–20:00, Mon–Sat. Async feedback within 48h (often 24h). Speaking Clubs are Tue/Fri/Sat.
